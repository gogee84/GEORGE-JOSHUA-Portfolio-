About

Self-taught, detail-first, building in public.

I'm George Joshua Monah — most people know me as Gogee. I build for the web from Arusha, Tanzania, and I got here the hands-on way: two years as a school laboratory technician taught me to follow a procedure exactly and check every result before calling it done. That habit followed me straight into code.

Outside of class I taught myself HTML, CSS and JavaScript by shipping real projects instead of only following tutorials — the two sites in this portfolio are both live, both hosted on GitHub, and both built end to end by me.
